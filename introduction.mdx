Providence Road is a Baton Rouge nonprofit founded in 2018 by Jon Odenwald (Coach J') and Cole Erno with a single, urgent question at its center: *how do you make a student care?* Working with at-risk youth ages 10–16 in North Baton Rouge, the organization offers trauma-informed care and engaging programming that establishes meaningful, safe relationships between adults and young people — relationships designed to support each child's social, spiritual, and emotional development. The premise is straightforward but profound: community change starts with the individual, and changing one life at a time is how you change a neighborhood, a city, and a future.

## Who They Serve

Providence Road shows up in some of Baton Rouge's most underserved neighborhoods, meeting youth where they are — physically, emotionally, and spiritually. The young people Providence Road serves carry stories shaped by poverty, trauma, and survival. Many have never been given a reason to invest in their own community, let alone believe that their community would invest in them. Providence Road exists to change that calculus, one relationship at a time.

> "I just want to thank Providence Road. We are very thankful for people coming to bad neighborhoods to help. The love they show these guys in the group is heartwarming to people in this community."
>
> — Jarvis, age 14

## What Makes Providence Road Different

Most interventions ask youth to change their behavior. Providence Road starts earlier — asking how you shift the *mindset* that drives behavior in the first place. Their answer is built around the motto **"Doing hard things in beautiful places changes lives."** By inviting young people into challenging, meaningful experiences alongside caring adults, they create the conditions where real transformation becomes possible.

The organization prioritizes building a track record: soft skills, community ownership, and the kind of trust that earns credibility with families, teachers, employers, and neighbors. The goal is not compliance — it is genuine investment in a young person's own story.
