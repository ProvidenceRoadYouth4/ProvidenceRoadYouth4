Providence Road meets youth where they live — literally. Three afternoons a week, staff and volunteers step into North Baton Rouge neighborhoods to offer programming that moves through five stages: engaging the community, building connection, practicing strength, empowering youth, and stepping into greatness. Every activity — whether picking up trash on a Tuesday evening, reading Frederick Douglass on a Wednesday, or taking a first solo trip out of state — is a rung on that ladder. After five years and over 75 youth impacted, the results speak: vacant lots cleared, confidence rebuilt, and young men writing poetry they never knew was inside them.

## Community Programs

Providence Road's community programs run weekly in North Baton Rouge and are the heartbeat of the organization. They are free to participate in and open to youth ages 10–16. Each program is intentionally designed to build relationship, responsibility, and the courage to do hard things.

### Gym and Fitness Trips

Beyond Tuesday and Wednesday, Providence Road takes youth to local gyms and fitness facilities throughout the year. In a single summer, the organization logs more than **150 trips** — a steady rhythm of physical challenge, healthy competition, and mentorship from coaches like Coach Dave, who says of the youth: *"I am these kids. I lived their story."* The gym is a place where tough becomes strong.

### Out-of-Town Service Experiences

One of the most powerful elements of the Providence Road model is crossing the boundary of what's familiar. Youth earn the opportunity to serve beyond Baton Rouge — joining dining hall crews at Camp Blessing in Brenham, TX, working on a camp service crew in Oregon, and more. Being off-balance in a new place, with trusted adults alongside them, accelerates growth in ways that no weekly session alone can replicate.

## School Programs

Providence Road's school-based programs are free for educators to adopt and bring the same culture of affirmation and honest inquiry that drives the community work directly into classrooms. Both programs use the **N.V.I.T.E.S. Arc™** — Notice, Value, Inquire, Trade, Enjoy, Share — as a shared practice framework.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card
    title="Big Steps™"
    icon="stairs"
    href="/programs/big-steps"
  >
    A student-led peer-affirmation practice for grades 4–12. Students name "first-time" growth actions and affirm one another in structured, 8–45 minute sessions that fit advisory, homeroom, or team time.
  </Card>
  <Card
    title="Chasing Understanding®"
    icon="magnifying-glass"
    href="/programs/chasing-understanding"
  >
    A structured reading practice for grades 6–12 that teaches students to notice confusion, name it out loud, and work through it together — flipping the classroom norm from pretending to understand to owning the moment you don't.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
