Confidence is contagious. It helps when others have it — and then seeing it, you start to think: *I can do that.* Even when you stumble, that feeling of confidence and purpose leads you into the adventure of your life and reveals the truth about who you are and what you are capable of. That is the principle Providence Road built Summer 2023 around. And no one demonstrated it more fully than CJ.

## The Solo Trip

CJ is one of Providence Road's senior members — a youth who has come up through the program, put in the years, and earned the trust of the Jump Team. In the summer of 2023, Providence Road made a decision: CJ was ready for something bigger.

They sent him to Oregon to spend three weeks working on a camp service crew. Alone. His first solo trip out of state. No familiar neighborhood. No group around him. Just CJ, a new environment, hard work, and the character he had been building inside Providence Road.

CJ crushed the opportunity.

He came back to Baton Rouge expressing a confidence that was new and a determination that was contagious. Service had done what it always does with CJ — it woke him up. Back in North Baton Rouge, CJ stepped into something else: leadership. He began modeling what Providence Road looks like outside of the neighborhood, raising the standard for younger members, and leading the culture of the group.

> "Confidence is contagious. It helps when others have it, and then seeing it, we start to think 'I can do that.'"
>
> — Providence Road Summer Recap '23

## The Spark: Kyree's Story

While CJ was flying across the country, another story was unfolding closer to home. Kyree, age 13, was sitting in Wednesday book club — working through *The Narrative of Frederick Douglass*, *Treasure Island*, and *Robinson Crusoe* — when something shifted.

*"This doesn't make sense!"* Kyree said out loud.

In most environments, a young person admitting confusion is a risk. At Providence Road, it was a breakthrough. Kyree's willingness to stay in the struggle of not understanding, to push toward comprehension rather than retreat from it, is the same spark that unlocks the potential and capacity of the human spirit.

By the end of summer, Kyree had written this:

---

> *Worthy of happening, working towards success.*
> *Way over the horizon working to be the best.*
> *Steppin with affirmation and mastering it along the way.*
> *Gaining wisdom that would help me in a tremendous way.*
>
> *Creating healthy boundaries that won't leave my side.*
> *Bringing happiness into my life no more darkness ever seen.*
> *Dreams don't happen unless you do.*
> *Being confident that my life would be great*
> *leaving all the dark thoughts behind.*
>
> *Slowly being fueled up by what God wants Kyree to see,*
> *life will be peaceful as long as you walk with me.*
> *Face challenges and work with me.*
> *and be in the biggest picture the world has ever seen.*

— Kyree, age 13 (July 19, 2023)

## What CJ and Kyree Teach Us

When youth find someone who identifies their potential, takes the time to create opportunities for exploration, and provides a safe space to practice understanding, the entire community benefits. CJ's solo trip to Oregon and Kyree's poem written at book club are not separate stories — they are the same story at different moments on the same road: a young person in North Baton Rouge discovering that their life can be about more than survival.
