Kevin on Service Project 2020

Kevin, has been rolling hard with Providence Road for over two years, and is growing up in the program. It’s a huge win for the community when young men step from the street life, and submit to the process offered by Providence Road.

The following pictures highlight Kevin’s journey with Providence Road. It’s the stuff these youth need, taking them from ‘tough to strong.’ _Tough_ is necessary to survive on the streets of North BR, but strong is _for others_, and when you step into strong, it wakes you up.
