_New Talent, New Faces_

_Dear Freinds of Providence Road,_

Welcome to 2021 from Providence Road! We have hit the ground running and want to take a moment to praise our new team members' efforts! 

Providence Road benefits from the incredible contributions of talented individuals, both locally and nationally. Our highly skilled team is growing, and we wanted to share our excitement with you! 

We want to welcome Amy and Erik to the road officially!

![Amy+and+Jeremy+Pic+copy.jpg](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5bc14fe2fb182049bcf2bf49/1612288440619-21XFM6ZHI3XFHINGQ3GO/Amy%2Band%2BJeremy%2BPic%2Bcopy.jpg)

Amy Gore

Development Officer/Board of Directors

A vibrant, passionate, and engaging young woman with extensive experience managing successful non-profits throughout the Southeast, Amy is the driving force in Providence Road's development team. 

Amy is keeping us on track as we navigate new opportunities related to growing our program and has brought a sense of clarity and articulation to our vision. Her husband Jeremy and their new son Aaron are also an integral part of the providence road family and we are so excited to have them on the journey with us!

> ““Amy’s ability to knock out tasks, pick up the ball, and help us move forward has been a necessary addition to the program... and that’s an understatement! She is the best at what she does!” ”

— Coach J' (Jon Odenwald)

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5bc14fe2fb182049bcf2bf49/1612288519408-MMP4M52D4CTCJM7PCBEI/image-asset.jpeg)

**_Erik Sampson_**

_Director Of Innovation_

With professional experience ranging from the performing arts and arts management to education, youth development, and most recently, business operations, Erik is playing a vital part in the expansion of Providence Road as we take vital steps to solidify our program aims, objectives, and operational capacity. 

With Erik's help, Providence Road is growing and stepping into new territory as we look at future possibilities to serve our community and Baton Rouge's youth!

> ““The heart and the talent Erik brings to Providence Road is unparalleled.” ”

— Coach J' (Jon Odenwald)
