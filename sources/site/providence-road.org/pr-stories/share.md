### Heritage Ranch Teams up With Providence Road

![IMG_6619.jpeg](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5bc14fe2fb182049bcf2bf49/1615223705964-GDKUVEJYAN1HO2RM5RPV/IMG_6619.jpeg)

_This is…_

_PROVIDENCE ROAD_

Dear Friends of Providence Road, 

One of the best parts of sharing the road is the opportunity to share in the struggle with great people and to know that others are in it with you.

We had a tremendous service project last week. One for the books with a total haul of over **_1 Ton_** of trash removed from the community! 

We were incredibly grateful to Heritage Ranch for providing the extra muscle and a chance to share in the struggle with like-minded individuals committed to seeing our youth succeed.

Impact In 2021:

_Youth Service Hours 
_**697 HOURS**

_Trash Removed From The Neighborhood 
_**7.7 TONS** 
_Meals Provided To Youth 
_**244**

_City Costs Saved 
_**$18,958**

While much of what we do is getting these youth to recognize the reward for showing up strong in their community, a huge part is introducing them to the concept of community. 

A community that won’t drop you at the first sign of trouble but a community willing to get down in the struggle.

— Bri Marines (Heritage Ranch)
