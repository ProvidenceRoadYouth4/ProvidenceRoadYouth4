Every session costs something. Every meal, every service project supply run, every gym trip, every out-of-town experience that a youth earns by building a track record — all of it takes real resources. Your donation is what makes it possible for Providence Road to keep showing up in North Baton Rouge neighborhoods, week after week, season after season, for the youth who need consistent adult presence most. When you give to Providence Road, you aren't funding a program. You're funding a relationship that might be the turning point in a young person's story.

> "The young men we deal with are living in crime-infested and impoverished areas. They are constantly surrounded by crime and opportunities to get involved with it. My main passion is getting in front of that process and disrupting the sequence that sucks these guys into bad choices."
>
> — George Gillam, Community Liaison

## What Your Donation Funds

<Accordion title="Why monthly giving matters">
  One of the greatest challenges for a youth-serving nonprofit isn't a single bad fundraising month — it's the unpredictability of boom-and-bust funding cycles. When Providence Road doesn't know what next quarter's budget looks like, programming suffers. Activities get cut, trips get cancelled, and the consistency that youth depend on gets disrupted.

  Monthly giving solves this. When a base of recurring contributors commits to giving a set amount every month — even a small amount — Providence Road can plan sessions, order supplies, and lock in out-of-town experiences weeks in advance. That reliability is the same thing Providence Road asks of its volunteers: just keep showing up.

  For the youth Providence Road serves, reliable adult presence is transformative precisely because it's rare. Monthly giving is the financial equivalent of showing up every Tuesday. It tells the organization — and through it, the youth — that you're in this for the long haul.
</Accordion>
