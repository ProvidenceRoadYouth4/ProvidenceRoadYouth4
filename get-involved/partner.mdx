Providence Road was never meant to be a solo effort. The work of guiding, nurturing, and strengthening the hearts and minds of youth in poverty is too big for a small team and too important to leave to chance. Organizational partners — churches, schools, civic nonprofits, businesses, camps, and ranches — bring muscle, resources, perspective, and new relationships into the struggle. When your organization joins Providence Road, your people get to experience what happens when adults who care work alongside youth who are discovering what they're capable of. Everyone changes. That's the point.

<Note>
  Providence Road is always on the lookout for talented, passionate individuals and organizations to join the journey. If your mission overlaps with serving youth in under-resourced communities, there is a place for you in this work.
</Note>

## Partnerships in Action

### Heritage Ranch

Heritage Ranch is the model for what an organizational partnership with Providence Road looks like at its best. When Heritage Ranch brought their team to join a Providence Road Tuesday service project, the combined group removed over one ton of trash from North Baton Rouge neighborhoods in a single session — one of the largest single-project hauls in Providence Road's history.

> "Serving with Providence Road was amazing! It was such an honor to have our kids see other kids work hard and be respectful. We are excited for the opportunity for our boys to grow in new friendships, diversity, and character."
>
> — Bri Marines, Heritage Ranch

The Heritage Ranch partnership demonstrated something Providence Road sees again and again: when outside organizations bring their people into proximity with North Baton Rouge youth, both groups come away changed. The North Baton Rouge youth see other young people choosing strength and service. The visiting youth see a community and a group of young men they might never have encountered otherwise. The adults on both sides are reminded why they do this work.

### Louisiana Stormwater Coalition

The Louisiana Stormwater Coalition partnered with Providence Road on one of the most sustained and civically significant projects in the organization's history — the multi-month cleanup of Capitol Lake in Baton Rouge. Over the course of the partnership, Providence Road youth and coalition volunteers removed 328 tires, two toilets, a couch, two boats, and 50 bags of litter from the lake's shores and surrounding wetlands.

The partnership didn't stop at cleanup. Providence Road youth accompanied coalition leader Marie Constantin to the State Legislature, where they witnessed a resolution to study stormwater litter-capture funding being proposed — and passed. For youth from North Baton Rouge, seeing themselves as part of civic change at the state level is exactly the kind of horizon-expanding experience Providence Road aims to provide.

## What an Organizational Partnership Looks Like

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Join Tuesday Service Projects" icon="people-carry-box" href="/get-involved/volunteer">
    Bring a group from your organization to join Providence Road youth on Tuesday evening neighborhood cleanups. No prior experience needed — just a willingness to work and show up for youth.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Provide Supplies or Funding" icon="boxes-stacked" href="/get-involved/donate">
    Supply donations — gloves, trash bags, tools, food — and financial contributions go directly to programming. Even a single supply run makes a Tuesday evening service project possible.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Collaborate on Out-of-Town Trips" icon="van-shuttle" href="/get-involved/impact">
    Ranches, camps, and outdoor programs can partner to host Providence Road youth for earned service experiences outside of Baton Rouge. These trips are among the most powerful experiences in the program.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Support School Programs" icon="school" href="/programs/big-steps">
    Schools can adopt Big Steps™ and Chasing Understanding® — Providence Road's formal school-based programs — with support from the Jump Team. No organizational affiliation required.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
