Providence Road exists for youth who are hard to reach — young people ages 10–16 in North Baton Rouge who often don't fit the mold of traditional youth programs. If you know a young person in this age range who is struggling, disconnected, or simply not being served by the programs that exist, Providence Road may be exactly the right place for them. Registration is free, the programming costs nothing to families, and the door is open to youth wherever they are in their story — no prerequisites, no behavior requirements, no expectations beyond showing up.

<Tip>
  Providence Road specifically seeks out youth who are hard to reach — those who don't fit traditional program models are exactly who they want. If a young person in your life has been overlooked or turned away by other programs, that's a reason to reach out, not a reason to wait.
</Tip>

## Who Can Participate

Providence Road serves **youth ages 10–16** in North Baton Rouge, with a particular focus on young people in under-resourced neighborhoods who face real barriers — poverty, limited adult support, environmental instability — to making the choices that lead to a different future.

> "Most of the kids who would show up to programs weren't the 'troubled kids.' The kids who were struggling weren't in programs, you didn't see them, but that didn't mean they didn't have potential. It just meant they didn't fit the model."
>
> — Coach J' (Jon Odenwald), Co-Founder & Executive Director

Providence Road goes to youth — into their neighborhoods, on their streets — rather than waiting for youth to find their way to a program. That proximity is what makes it possible to reach young people who wouldn't otherwise have access to consistent adult investment and structured opportunity.

## No Cost to Youth or Families

Core Providence Road programming — service projects, book club, gym trips, mentorship — is provided **at no cost** to participating youth or their families. Providence Road is funded through individual donations, monthly contributors, and organizational partnerships. Your family pays nothing.

## How to Register

You have two options to get a young person enrolled:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register Online">
    Visit [providence-road.org/register](https://providence-road.org/register) to complete the youth registration form. You'll provide basic information about the young person and how to reach your family.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reach Out Directly">
    Email [providenceroad.jtf@gmail.com](mailto:providenceroad.jtf@gmail.com) with the youth's name, age, and your contact information. The Jump Team will follow up to walk you through next steps and let you know when and where to show up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Youth Don't Need to Be "Ready"

Providence Road does not require youth to be well-behaved, academically on track, or emotionally stable before joining. The Cultivation Model meets youth where they are and walks them — patiently, consistently — toward strength. Youth who have been through hard things, who have a difficult track record, who have been told they are too much to handle: those are the young people Providence Road is built for.

The only thing that matters at the start is that a young person is willing to show up. Providence Road will take it from there.
